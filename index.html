A Letter of Devotion

In the Manner of Kings and Queens

In the most pleasant and respectful way I bow for you. You are the sunshine of my life, you make me thrive and ride a roalercoaster of happiness. Your smile and love is the only emotion I like to see. I think of ourselves as how kings and Queens used to live in the good old medieval victorian times. I here stand to present my respect and love to you. You make me happy person. I despise the thought of us getting separated. I would love and choose in the fierciest storms and be by your side. Regards Izzan
